

Part C — Requirements capture worksheet



Case A: Kinda Ridge Primary School, Port Moresby 



Write shall-statements where possible. Quality beats quantity — but hit the minimums.

C1. Stakeholders (min 3)

Who, and what they need from the system

Bursar/Accounts Officer	- Wants to record fee payments accurately, track instalments or installments, then generate reports … yeah, keep it all consistent.

School Principal - Sees fee collection summaries, plus any outstanding balances, and checks whether everything looks right.

Students - Make sure their fee payments are recorded correctly in the system, so nothing gets missed.









C2. Functional requirements (min 5, numbered FR-1…)

The system shall …

&#x20;FR-1 The system shall record the updates regarding the student fees payment.

&#x20;FR-2 The system shall maintain a history of all payment transactions.

&#x20;FR-3 The system shall record fee payments received by cash, bank deposit, and mobile money.

&#x20;FR-4 The system shall allow authorized staff to search and update student fee records.

&#x20;FR-5 The system should produce reports showing paid and unpaid student accounts.







C3. Non-functional requirements (min 3, think PNG: offline, power, usability)

NFR-1 …

&#x20;NFR-1 The system should be easy to use and require minimal training for office staff.

&#x20;NFR-2 The System should save data automatically to prevent data loss during power fluctuations. 

&#x20;NFR-3 The system should work offline when internet connectivity is Unavailable.











C4. Constraints \& business rules (min 2 each)

Constraints (facts of the environment) / Rules (policies to enforce)



&#x20;Constraints 

\-	The internet is unreliable and may not be available all times. 

\-	Power Interruptions may occur during working hours. 



Business Rules 

\-	Each student shall have a unique student identification number.

\-	Outstanding balances shall be updated immediately after each payment.  



C5. Validation notes



What did the client correct when you played it back?

During the review of the requirements, the client confirmed the following points and provided a few corrections



\- Payments are received through cash, bank deposits, and mobile money and all methods must be supported. 

\-The bursar emphasized the need for printed receipts for every transaction. 

\-The school requires offline access because internet service is not always reliable. 

\-The principal requested reports showing outstanding balances for each term.
